## Anomalies and process notes
- **SDL SIGFPE / startup race** once in 13 launches (V1 try 1; `startup race try 1`), relaunched. Known quirk.
- **`/tmp` quota (EDQUOT)** was exhausted by other sessions' files at about 03:11Z (6.2 GB used of 7.7 GB, mostly under `/tmp/claude-1000`); with it, `grim` (PNG/JPEG buffers), `wtype` ("no memory") and every Bash result failed for about 8 minutes. With the lead's approval I deleted only the 65 `/tmp/cast150_*` directories not modified in the last 60 minutes (about 369 MB) and nothing else. After that the pass continued; all my scratch and screenshots are under `.local/onscreen/`. Two bursts taken during the outage were discarded (empty frames); the results above are from later bursts.
- One of my own background Bash tasks (a Resume burst) stalled on the quota; I stopped it with `TaskStop`, the game it had launched exited normally (code 143, 0 errors; `logs` not kept), and I relaunched it.
- `grim -t jpeg` fails ("failed to create buffer") under heavy memory pressure while the game runs (about 13.6 GB used of 15.7 GB); `-t ppm` with a region works, which is what the bursts use.
- A near-zoom ranged attempt from the move tile turned out adjacent to the archer and played Melee (by design: Ranged only from more than one cell); I redid it from three cells (`sheets/05`, `06`).
