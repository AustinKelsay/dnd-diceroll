## 3. Anomalies

- **SDL SIGFPE at launch, 1 of 8 launches** (`logs/game-g4-SIGFPE-startup-try1.log`, `handle_wl_output_done`, `SDL_waylandvideo.c:1016`): the known headless-output race, my helper relaunched. No crash in any played session; no crash report.
- **Unexplained turn advance (low confidence, cannot reproduce).** In the Vault finale at round 1, while I only sent camera input (wheel, `E`/`Q`, a left-button drag that I should have made a right-drag, a middle-drag), Arden's turn and the other party turns passed and two heroes were downed by the enemy turn (round 2 when I looked). A later test of `Q`x3, `E`x3 and wheel changed no state. I cannot rule out my rig (the stray left-drag clicks) and did not isolate it. Worth one human check that camera keys never end a turn.
- **Rejected commands after a right-drag orbit**: the keyboard cursor mapping follows the camera yaw, so my bot's arrow keys missed after I orbited (expected behaviour, my rig). A fresh launch plus Resume restored the default yaw.
- **A long stalemate in enc 2** (my bot, not a human): behind a broken wall with a one-cell gap a cleric and a wizard at 1 HP were boxed in; rescue gives back 1 HP and the raider downed the target again. About 20 rounds before the bot routed around the wall. The rules allowed it; a human would not have played it, but it shows rescue restores only 1 HP.
- Every game: 0 `Error:` lines and 0 `LogMaterial: Warning`. No stale frame, flicker or missing texture noticed in the played frames.
