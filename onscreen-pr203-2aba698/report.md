## Recommendation
**Merge, with two notes for the lead.** On the package built from `2aba698`: perf passes with the same margin as #201 for the default party, a bard and artificer party and a four-fighter party (3.34 to 3.36 / 2.67 to 2.69 / 1.17 to 1.20 ms, about 1248 MiB), with no long frame in the fight. The bard and artificer read and play correctly (teal bolt with Cast, rapier close up, lightless crossbow arrow, a cyan bolt only for the damaging spell special; no caster blowout, white max 0.53%); the six caster bolts are six clearly different hues; the 70 builds across all 18 palette parties read as distinct, non-garish versions of their class, the Hunter and Swarmkeeper clearly differ; rescue and Resume show no pops and the fighters' accents are identical after Resume; the default party and the foe arrows are as before.
1. **The accents do not meet "told apart at a glance" for every class**: fighters (shield face) and clerics do; **paladins are marginal and rogues are not distinguishable at default zoom** (four rogues look identical). If the owner wants the gate to hold for every class, the rogue and paladin accent regions are too small at 70 px (consider the rogue's cloak or hood trim and the paladin's tabard hem), but the accents do not hurt anything and the PR's own measure (5.6% and 8.1% of the figure) is the cause.
2. Wizard amber and cleric gold are the closest bolt pair.
