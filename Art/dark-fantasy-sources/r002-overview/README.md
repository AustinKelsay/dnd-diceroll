# The overview set r002: war table and route dioramas (issue #149, parent #138)

What the title screen and the route choice show behind the HUD: a candlelit war table with the three route dioramas on it. Four new assets (all new packages
under `/Game/DnDDiceRoll/Theme/Overview`; the r001 `SM_Tabletop`, `SM_Diorama_Root` and `SM_Diorama_Vigil` stay as the fallback), original and procedural,
generated by the scripts in `scripts/` in Blender 5.2.3 LTS.

```sh
blender -b --python Art/dark-fantasy-sources/r002-overview/scripts/build_tabletop_r002.py      # SM_Overview_Tabletop + tabletop_metrics.json + atlas png
blender -b --python Art/dark-fantasy-sources/r002-overview/scripts/build_dioramas_r002.py      # [-- root|vigil|vault] the three dioramas + diorama_metrics.json
blender -b --python Art/dark-fantasy-sources/r002-overview/scripts/render_previews_overview.py # previews/ (game camera, HUD blocks drawn in grey)
blender -b --python Art/dark-fantasy-sources/r002-overview/scripts/survey_worlds.py            # previews/survey_*.png: height maps used to choose the windows
python3 Art/dark-fantasy-sources/r002-overview/scripts/write_records_overview_r002.py          # asset.json for these and for world-vigil-occluders r002
```

Under the machine lock on the shared host. Needs `Art/dark-fantasy-sources/r002-rose` (the patched Vigil occluders) first.

## What the frame shows (why it is composed this way)

The overview camera (eye 420 cm back and 208 cm above the table top, aimed 32 degrees down, FOV 50) and the HUD together hide most of an ordinary table: on the route screen the banner and the two
route cards cover the middle of the frame, and on the start screen the class panel covers the left third. What stays visible is the strip above the banner, the flanks and the band below the
cards, and on the start screen everything between the panels. The r001 table was 120 cm deep, so the lower half of the frame was bare floor and the dioramas sat behind the banner. The r002 table is 265 cm deep
(front edge at the bottom of the frame), the war map and the props live in the band below the cards, the three candles flank the dioramas and the right flank holds a tankard, dagger, dice and coins.
`previews/game_start.png`, `game_route.png` and `game_finale.png` draw the HUD blocks in grey over the Blender render of this camera.

## The war table `SM_Overview_Tabletop`

Origin at the middle of the table top, the asset faces +X (the overview turns it -90 degrees about Z), +Y is the viewer's right. 3.4 x 2.65 m oak planks at 256 texels/m (a 512 px, 2 m tile), a leather-spined bookcase behind it, a hand-drawn
vellum map (640 x 384 px on 1.05 x 0.63 m, about 610 texels/m) of the five-stop route with a fork, held by stone weights with five glowing route markers (vertex-alpha glow), a brass candlestick, a pillar candle and a candle cluster
(the three runtime lights sit on these flames: `tabletop_metrics.json` `lights_m`), a tankard, dagger, d20, d8 and d6 dice, coins, a pouch, inkwell and quill, scrolls, a spyglass, a book stack, a letter and an hourglass; shelf candle jars.
Conforming subdivision (3.5 cm near the props, 10 cm on the map), ray-cast ambient occlusion and a warm candle bake into `COLOR_0`; flames and route markers carry glow in `COLOR_0.a`. One 2048 atlas, 37 % filled.

## The dioramas `SM_Overview_Diorama_{Root,Vigil,Vault}`

Each is the real r002 world cropped to a window with exact clipping, scaled to a 58 cm tray (about 1:48 to 1:66), vertically exaggerated (x1.5 to x1.8, a miniature reads better a little tall), with a few kit pieces drawn larger
than the world scale (the landmarks), on a dark-oak tray with brass corners and an engraved plaque (leaf, waves, rune). The world's baked vertex light and glow mask are kept; the texture is the world atlas shrunk to fit one 1024 atlas
(world x 1/4, occluders x 1/8 or 1/16, kit pieces x 1/8). The Root is the clearing (spires, stump, boulder, glow mushrooms, root tangles); the Vigil is the nave with its tall walls, lancets and the rose (from the new high-resolution atlas) and
columns; the Vault is the crypt hall with the Waystone in its apse, ossuary pier, stele, stacked sarcophagi, rune halo and crystals. Orientation is a rotation of the world's frame that puts its tall back architecture away from the viewer (never a mirror).

| | Root | Vigil | Vault |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Window (m, glb frame) | 28 x 20 | 38 x 36 | 36 x 26 |
| Scale | 1:48 | 1:66 | 1:62 |
| Triangles | 11,246 | 17,996 | 29,346 |
| Size (m) | 0.49 x 0.65 x 0.24 | 0.62 x 0.65 x 0.52 | 0.49 x 0.65 x 0.26 |

The Vault diorama exists because the overview offers the finale (encounter 5): `BuildOverview` drew the Vigil diorama there before.

## Previews and limits

`previews/` are Blender EEVEE renders with a stand-in light (three warm point lights at the flames, a dim cool fill, no grade, no shadows beyond EEVEE's): `game_nohud.png`, `game_start.png`, `game_route.png`, `game_finale.png`, `table_wide.png`, one close view per diorama.
Whether the dioramas are bright enough, and how much of them the banner hides, is judged in the game's capture (see the PR). The mid-table is bare on purpose: the party's miniatures stand there.
