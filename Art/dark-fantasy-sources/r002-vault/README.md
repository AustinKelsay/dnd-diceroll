# The Vault world kit r002 (issue #149, parent #138)

The finale's own world, built to the standard #145 and the Root r002 set (150 cm cell, nothing in the board footprint, a camera clear volume, an occluder split, one 2048² atlas at about 256 texels/m, baked vertex light, explicit LODs, Nanite off). Until now the finale (encounter 5) drew the Drowned Vigil kit with a cold grade. Everything here is original, generated by the scripts in `scripts/` in Blender 5.2.3 LTS; no outside content, kits, paid or AI services. Dark high fantasy with a low-poly PS1 influence, smooth and sharp, not grainy.

**Identity:** a roofless crypt hall of cold blue-black basalt ashlar, bone, rusted iron and one glow accent, the cold blue (0.5, 0.85, 1.0) the finale's text already uses, carried only by the vertex-alpha glow mask (`rune` and `crystal`). It reads against the Vigil by structure (a walled, paved hall with alcoves and a landmark, not a flooded cathedral) and by palette (blue-black stone, bone and a single cyan accent, no warm stone).

Conventions (as #145 and Root): metres, +X right, **+Y away from the camera's home side** (home yaw is -90, so the front is -Y), Z up (glTF Y-up export), 1 cell = 150 cm, the 18 x 12 cell footprint is 27 x 18 m centred on the origin (the 16 x 11 board sits inside it). Every glb has one material, one baseColor atlas and `COLOR_0` as `VEC4` normalised uint16: RGB = baked light, A = glow mask on world and props (1 only on `rune` and `crystal` triangles, 0 elsewhere) or opacity on mist and shaft cards. The sampler is Linear/mipmapped, never nearest.

## Rebuild

From the repository root, one Blender process at a time (and under the machine lock on the shared host):

```sh
blender -b --python Art/dark-fantasy-sources/r002-vault/scripts/build_vault_r002.py       # world + occluders + atlas + world_metrics.json (~15 s)
blender -b --python Art/dark-fantasy-sources/r002-vault/scripts/build_kit_vault_r002.py   # obstacle kit, arch, scatter, mist, shafts + kit_metrics.json
blender -b --python Art/dark-fantasy-sources/r002-vault/scripts/build_lods_vault_r002.py  # LOD1/LOD2 glbs (nodes named LODn_SM_X) + lod_metrics.json
python3 Art/dark-fantasy-sources/r002-vault/scripts/relabel_lods_vault_r002.py            # strips Blender's .001 suffix from LOD names, refreshes the hashes
blender -b --python Art/dark-fantasy-sources/r002-vault/scripts/validate_vault_r002.py    # fresh-process file, footprint, sight-volume, eye and ray checks -> camera_check.json (~1 min)
blender -b --python Art/dark-fantasy-sources/r002-vault/scripts/validate_lods_vault_r002.py
python3 Art/dark-fantasy-sources/r002-vault/scripts/write_records_vault_r002.py           # asset.json for every export
blender -b --python Art/dark-fantasy-sources/r002-vault/scripts/render_previews_vault.py [-- shot ...]
python3 Art/dark-fantasy-sources/r002-vault/scripts/make_sheets_vault_r002.py             # previews/contact_sheet.png (ImageMagick 7)
```

Scripts: `r002_lib.py`, `lod_r002.py` and `camera_volume.py` (the #145/Root helpers, copied; `lod_r002.py` gains pinned-sharp-edge decimation and the Vault LOD limits), `vault_lib.py` (textures and geometry: ashlar, pilasters, rib arcs, sarcophagi, statues, chains, bones, urns, crystals), `paving_vault.py` (the Root paving recipe in cold blue-grey), `build_vault_r002.py`, `build_kit_vault_r002.py`, `build_lods_vault_r002.py`, `validate_*`, `relabel_lods_vault_r002.py`, `write_records_vault_r002.py`, `render_previews_vault.py`, `make_sheets_vault_r002.py`. Outputs: `world_metrics.json`, `kit_metrics.json`, `lod_metrics.json`, `camera_check.json`, `T_World_Vault_Atlas.png`, `previews/`.

## Layout (agreed with #188's encounter-identity contract)

- **Front open.** The camera's home side (-Y) has no wall: the side walls run out as broken, declining runs that end 1.7 m before the board's centre line and give way to rubble, sarcophagus rows, a collapsed vault rib, drums and chains.
- **Walls 1-2 cells outside the board** at the back and sides: inner faces at x = ±16.5 m and y = 12.1 m, which is 2.0 and 2.07 cells outside the 18 x 12 board and 3.0 and 2.6 outside the 16 x 11 board. They cannot come closer for the largest board: the braziers stand 1.25 cells out (1.9 m) and the pilasters (30 cm proud) must clear them. The walls are therefore at the outer edge of "1-2 cells" for 18 x 12 and beyond it for the smaller boards, and the world is one fixed mesh.
- **Waystone landmark** centred on the back wall, in an apse (a 4.6 m wide recess 2.4 m deep) on a three-step dais: a tapered basalt monolith 6.0 m high with cold-blue rune channels, a halo of twelve rune segments floating before its head (the far-zoom signature) and an inlaid rune ring; two hooded statues kneel at the steps.
- **Braziers** stay in the game's ring (1.25 cells out); dressing goes 2-5 cells out. Nothing above 3.3 cm stands inside the 27 x 18 m footprint (the paving's relief), and the game keeps its own scatter two cells out.

## The camera clear volume

As Root (`camera_volume.py`, #143's pose law): the **sight volume** is the convex hull of the footprint and every camera eye of the envelope; the world shell stays 1.0 m outside it; meshes that enter it are the occluders (`SM_World_Vault_Occluders`: the three wall runs, the corbel stubs, the hanging chains and the collapsed rib), which the game dithers when they block the camera. **Eye space:** no mesh comes within 1.5 m of an eye.

| Check | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Highest point inside the 27 x 18 m footprint (world / occluders) | 3.3 cm / 0 cm (limit 10 cm) |
| World sample points inside the sight volume (+0.5 m), hull-surface overlaps | 0, 0 |
| Occluder sample points inside it, hull-surface overlaps | 108,176 (by design), 2,412 |
| Eyes (22,824) closer than 1.5 m to any geometry, or behind a surface | 0, 0 (nearest beyond 2.0 m) |
| 72 cameras x 864 floor targets (62,208 rays) blocked by the world / touching an occluder | 0 / 353 |
| Envelope sweep (848 eyes x 54 targets = 45,792 rays) blocked by the world | 0 |

## Paving

The Root recipe (irregular flagstones in wavy, interlocking Voronoi patches, never aligned to the cells) in cold blue-grey: 1,775 stones, equivalent diameter p5/p50/p95 41.7/66.1/82.7 cm, long side 44-101 cm, edge length within 3 degrees of the cell axes 0.5 %, R6 = 0.046, stone tops 57 % of the footprint, 4 % bare patches (a crypt is paved wall to wall). The stone is darker and bluer than the first pass (albedo about 0.21-0.30), joints and floor between the stones are a dark slate with grit, so figures do not swim in a bright carpet. Judge it in game with the characters' Rim and Fill (#148).

## LODs

`<name>_LOD1.glb` (about 50 %) and `<name>_LOD2.glb` (about 25 %, up to 40 % where the gates need it) beside the LOD0 of every mesh over about 1k triangles and the obstacle variants and arch (as Root). Differences: **the occluders have LOD1 only** (54 %): their box-built architecture pins its sharp-edge vertices (a plain collapse spans the alcoves by 0.5 m), so a LOD2 would equal LOD1; **limits:** world 6 cm (the paving is 5.4 cm from grout to stone top, so a collapsed paving triangle may sit 5.1 cm from LOD0), kit LOD1 7 cm, LOD2 20 cm; kit LODs pin glow vertices so the glow area survives. `validate_lods_vault_r002.py` re-opens each LOD in a fresh process: triangle target, UVs, RGBA alpha range, glow area, Linear sampler, atlas pixels, bounding box, surface distance both ways, appearance error, and for the world nothing above 10 cm inside the footprint and the footprint-edge region within 3 cm.

## Known limits, to judge in Unreal

- The Blender renders do not include the game's grade, fog, exposure, shadows or the characters' Rim and Fill. The Vault's post settings are tuned in game (see `docs/architecture.md`, battle camera and lighting).
- The far hall is a plain ashlar ring with buttresses and a few lancet glows; it is visible only at low zoom and from the back yaws.
- Walls are 2.0-3.0 cells out for the largest to the smallest board (see Layout).
- The chains in the occluders are one connected beaded tube (not separate links) so the mesh decimates; the floor chains are separate links.
