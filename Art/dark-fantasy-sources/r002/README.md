# Drowned Vigil world kit r002 (issue #145, parent #138)

Blender half; the Unreal import (reviewed reimport, Nanite off, explicit LODs, trilinear/aniso 8) is phase 2 (`Content/Python/import_dark_fantasy_theme.py`, `theme_plan.py`). Nothing here was imported into Unreal and no editor was run by the Blender work. Everything is original, generated by the scripts in `scripts/` in Blender 5.2.3 LTS from the r001 sources (`../r001/vigil.blend` and its libraries, which are read, never modified). No outside content. The [#140 spike](https://github.com/AustinKelsay/dnd-diceroll/tree/95cf93c4e18a4048ad4e7eda292d2a575a264a18/Art/spike-140) code was reused as an approach and copied into new scripts; its branch is not merged.

Conventions: metres, +X forward, Z up (glTF Y-up export), 1 cell = 150 cm, 18 x 12 cells = 27 x 18 m centred on the origin. Every glb has one material, one baseColor atlas and `COLOR_0` as `VEC4` normalised uint16: RGB = baked light, A = glow mask (flames, runes, glass) on world and kit pieces, or opacity on mist and shaft cards. Sampler is Linear/mipmapped, never nearest.

## Rebuild

Run from the repository root, one Blender process at a time (the world takes about 10 s; peak memory was not measured):

```sh
blender -b --python Art/dark-fantasy-sources/r002/scripts/build_vigil_r002.py   # world shell + occluders + atlas + world_metrics.json
blender -b --python Art/dark-fantasy-sources/r002/scripts/build_kit_r002.py     # obstacle kit, scatter, mist/shaft + kit_metrics.json
blender -b --python Art/dark-fantasy-sources/r002/scripts/build_lods_r002.py       # LOD1/LOD2 glbs beside the LOD0 exports + lod_metrics.json (reads the LOD0 glbs, never changes them)
blender -b --python Art/dark-fantasy-sources/r002/scripts/validate_lods_r002.py    # fresh-process check of every LOD
python3 Art/dark-fantasy-sources/r002/scripts/write_records_r002.py             # asset.json for every export incl. LODs (rerun after any rebuild)
blender -b --python Art/dark-fantasy-sources/r002/scripts/validate_r002.py      # re-opens every glb: UVs, RGBA, sampler, footprint, atlas identity
blender -b --python Art/dark-fantasy-sources/r002/scripts/render_previews_r002.py [-- shot ...]
python3 Art/dark-fantasy-sources/r002/scripts/make_sheets_r002.py               # previews/contact_sheet.png (ImageMagick 7)
```

Files: `scripts/r002_lib.py` (shared helpers: conforming subdivision, AO bake, periodic paving tile, RGBA object and glb export, glb read-back), `build_vigil_r002.py`, `build_kit_r002.py`, `render_previews_r002.py`, `make_sheets_r002.py`, `write_records_r002.py`, `validate_r002.py`, and for the LODs `lod_r002.py` (helpers), `build_lods_r002.py`, `validate_lods_r002.py`. Outputs: `world_metrics.json`, `kit_metrics.json`, `T_World_Vigil_Atlas.png` (the world atlas, identical pixels in both world glbs), `previews/`.

The LODs are made by Blender's collapse decimator from the LOD0 glb data (`build_lods_r002.py`): UV-island borders are kept as seams, 16-bit `COLOR_0` (RGB light, A glow) and the atlas pass through unchanged, and the vertices on the bounding box faces are pinned so the footprint and silhouette extremes never shrink. Most of the saving undoes the conforming subdivision (coplanar triangles), so the deviation is millimetres; the small kit pieces at LOD2 deviate by up to 17 cm (stump and drums). `validate_lods_r002.py` re-opens each LOD in a fresh Blender process: triangle target, UVs in 0..1, RGBA alpha range and glow area equal to LOD0, Linear sampler, identical atlas pixels, bounding box, surface distance both ways (5 cm world; 5 cm LOD1 and 20 cm LOD2 kit), texture x vertex colour error (mean <= 0.04), and for the world and occluders nothing above 10 cm inside the 27 x 18 m footprint and the footprint-edge region within 3 cm of LOD0 (measured 0.5 mm LOD1, 8 mm LOD2). Counts and errors are in `lod_metrics.json` and the asset records.

## What changed against the spike and r001

- **Paving (revised after lead review):** the Voronoi stones read as a hexagon lattice, so they were replaced by irregular flagstones: 5.5 units (4 m) patches, each laid in courses of random height with random stone lengths, bond offsets, drifting course edges, slanted joints, perturbed and chamfered corners, and its own random angle (never within 12 degrees of the cell axes). Joints are 4.5 cm and only slightly darker than the stones, with moss concentrated on stone edges and in joints in broken patches. A spike bug that culled some stone tops (5 triangles per edge, not 6) is fixed, and a 5 cm skirt closes terrace edges.
- **Occluders:** conforming midpoint subdivision (edges are split per geometric edge, so neighbours split together, no T-junctions), coplanar duplicate triangles removed (86, they also made Blender flag the mesh invalid), and an adaptive limit (75 cm below 4 m, 2.25 m above). 41.6k became 12.3k.
- **Terraces:** a seamless 512 px flagstone tile with moss from vertex colour, instead of r001's squares.
- **Rune ring** moved to the terrace, clear of the playable area.
- **Textures:** Linear sampling is declared in the material; atlas padding is 16 px (world) or 8 px (kit) instead of 4.

## Known limits

- Nothing was imported or rendered in Unreal. LOD screen sizes are the engine's defaults until tuned in Unreal. Whether the engine reads `COLOR_0.a`, the draw-call and frame cost of a 118k-triangle one-material world, and the real material look are unverified.
- The world mesh is one 136 x 143 m object (r001's outer architecture is tall, up to 48 m). About 82k of its triangles are flat outer terrace, which could be merged or simplified; there is no culling split or LOD.
- Occluders are 55 whole components, not instanced pieces. Their pivots are the world origin. The dither is per pixel (a board test, plus the sight lines; see `docs/assets.md`), so a piece fades only where it covers the board. The 0-residual occlusion re-test above covers the world shell at 72 cameras; the in-game camera fit is wider (far yaw 180 on 18 x 12), so confirm in the gate-2 captures that no world-shell geometry blocks the board.
- Baked light is the r001 light resampled plus ray-cast AO; there is no new direct-light bake and no directional AO term. Stone tops carry one light value per ring vertex, so shadows from the key light must come from the game.
- Architecture textures are r001's 128-256 px tiles, untouched; the 2048 atlas therefore gives no extra wall detail. Only paving and the terrace gained density.
- Kit atlases are per asset and duplicated across glbs (r001 convention), 128 to 512 px, with small tiles (62 to 213 texels/m); a shared kit atlas would save memory in Unreal.
- Obstacle footprints are inside 150 cm except a few rubble stones and the sword pommel (up to about 1.6 m wide); scatter props may extend slightly past the cell. The 2-cell arch is 1.47 m deep.
- The light shafts and mist cards are authored for a translucent, depth-write-off, two-sided card material; the previews' look of them is Blender's.
- The kit is simple faceted geometry in the PS1 look; no hand-sculpted detail, no bespoke texture painting beyond procedural soft noise. Variants share textures, so they read as one family rather than distinct silhouettes.

- Flagstone patch borders show as straight, slightly wider joints (slivers under 21 cm are dropped there); at the far pose they read as faint crack lines.
- **Gate 2 (round-1 review, nit):** the straight patch seams are left as they are (changing the paving changes the world glb, its LODs, hashes and the metric gate). Judge them in the gate-2 captures at the far pose; if they read as a grid, a seam-breaking pass (jittered patch borders) is a world re-export.
- LOD glbs name their mesh, node and material `LOD<n>_<name>` (Interchange's `LODxx_Name`); the file names stay `<name>_LOD<n>.glb`.
- Light shafts are now pale moonlight blue-white at peak opacity 0.07 (prism) and 0.06 (crossed planes); mist cards are unchanged.
