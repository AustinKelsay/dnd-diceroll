# Root of the World world kit r002 (issue #149, parent #138)

Blender half of the Root r002 slice, built to the standard #145 set for the Drowned Vigil. **Revision 2 (lead review of 5ef7dad):** the paving was recalibrated (value and contrast down, grey-green stone in earth, moss and flat roots) and its 4 m patch grid replaced by wavy, interlocking Voronoi patches; see Paving below. Nothing here was imported into Unreal and no editor was run. Everything is original, generated by the scripts in `scripts/` in Blender 5.2.3 LTS; the r001 Root geometry is not reused (r001's Root was a 106 m diorama whose giant tree stood 4 m behind the board and swallowed the HUD-safe camera), only its identity: the Root of the World, its roots, mossy flagstones, fungus and light falling through the canopy. The paving recipe started as #145's, copied into `scripts/paving_root.py` and extended (its branch, `feature/145-vigil-world-kit`, is not touched). No outside content, kits, paid or AI services.

Conventions (as #145): metres, +X forward, Z up (glTF Y-up export), 1 cell = 150 cm, the 18 x 12 cell footprint is 27 x 18 m centred on the origin (the board centre; the 16 x 11 board sits inside it). Every glb has one material, one baseColor atlas and `COLOR_0` as `VEC4` normalised uint16: RGB = baked light, A = glow mask (alpha 1 only on fungus caps and sap veins; 0 everywhere else) on world and props, or opacity on mist and shaft cards. The sampler is Linear/mipmapped, never nearest. (The brief said "everything else gets alpha 1"; the established r002 convention and `import_dark_fantasy_theme.py` read alpha as the glow mask, and a missing or constant-1 alpha would make a mesh glow, so non-glowing meshes carry 0.)

## Rebuild

From the repository root, one Blender process at a time:

```sh
blender -b --python Art/dark-fantasy-sources/r002-root/scripts/build_root_r002.py       # world + occluders + atlas + world_metrics.json (~30 s)
blender -b --python Art/dark-fantasy-sources/r002-root/scripts/build_kit_root_r002.py   # obstacle kit, arch, scatter, mist, shafts + kit_metrics.json
blender -b --python Art/dark-fantasy-sources/r002-root/scripts/build_lods_root_r002.py  # LOD1/LOD2 glbs (nodes named LODn_SM_X) + lod_metrics.json
python3 Art/dark-fantasy-sources/r002-root/scripts/relabel_lods_root_r002.py            # strips Blender's .001 suffix from LOD names, refreshes the hashes
blender -b --python Art/dark-fantasy-sources/r002-root/scripts/validate_root_r002.py    # fresh-process file, footprint, sight-volume, eye and ray checks -> camera_check.json (~2 min)
blender -b --python Art/dark-fantasy-sources/r002-root/scripts/validate_lods_root_r002.py
python3 Art/dark-fantasy-sources/r002-root/scripts/write_records_root_r002.py           # asset.json for every export
blender -b --python Art/dark-fantasy-sources/r002-root/scripts/measure_paving_root.py -- after   # paving luminance and figure readability -> paving_measure.json
blender -b --python Art/dark-fantasy-sources/r002-root/scripts/render_previews_root.py [-- shot ...]
python3 Art/dark-fantasy-sources/r002-root/scripts/make_sheets_root_r002.py             # previews/contact_sheet.png (ImageMagick 7)
```

Scripts: `r002_lib.py` and `lod_r002.py` (#145 helpers, copied; `lod_r002.py` gains an `organic` silhouette limit for the occluders), `camera_volume.py` (the camera law and clear volume), `root_lib.py` (textures, roots, trunks, stumps, logs, fungus, ferns, canopy), `paving_root.py`, `build_root_r002.py`, `build_kit_root_r002.py`, `build_lods_root_r002.py`, `validate_*`, `relabel_lods_root_r002.py`, `measure_paving_root.py`, `write_records_root_r002.py`, `render_previews_root.py`, `make_sheets_root_r002.py`. Outputs: `world_metrics.json`, `kit_metrics.json`, `lod_metrics.json`, `camera_check.json`, `T_World_Root_Atlas.png`, `previews/`.

## The camera clear volume

The camera (#143, `CameraRig.h`) orbits the board centre on an arm of 1.1768 x distance x (150/110), pitch tied to zoom (35 / 45 / 60 degrees), distance 900-6400 reference units. `camera_volume.py` computes every eye that law can produce (framed distance 1400-4600, 36 yaws, 317 reach/height pairs, up to 51 m out and 89 m up) plus the lead's envelope (reach 55 m, height 66 m, pitch 35-60). Two rules come out of it:

- **Sight volume** = the convex hull (724 planes) of the footprint plus a half-cell margin (lifted 12 cm) and every eye. Every sight line from any eye to any floor cell lies inside it. The world shell (`SM_World_Root`) stays 1.0 m outside it; only the ground within 12 cm of the floor level is allowed in (the ground bowl is capped under it). The hull has a maximum radius of 55 m, so tall trunks stand outside r = 56.5 m, while inside it masses are capped by the hull's lower surface (about 1.6 m at 18 m along X, 7 m at 24 m, 22 m at 40 m, 37 m at 56 m). Meshes that enter the volume are occluders (`SM_World_Root_Occluders`).
- **Eye space**: no mesh, world or occluder, contains an eye or comes within 1.5 m of one.

Verification (`validate_root_r002.py` re-opens the glbs in a fresh process; numbers in `camera_check.json`, and a negative control with a 4 x 4 x 10 m box inside the volume blocked 688 of the 72-camera rays and was flagged by the hull test):

| Check | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Highest point inside the 27 x 18 m footprint (world / occluders) | 5.2 cm (flat roots) / 0 cm (limit 10 cm) |
| World sample points inside the sight volume (+0.5 m), hull-surface overlaps | 0, 0 overlaps |
| Occluder sample points inside it, hull-surface overlaps | 10,448 (by design), 357 |
| Eyes (22,824: curve x 5 degree yaws) closer than 1.5 m to any geometry, or behind a surface | 0, 0 (nearest geometry beyond 2.0 m) |

| Envelope sweep, 848 eyes of the eye curve x 54 targets | 0 of 45,792 rays blocked by the world |

## Paving revision (lead review of 5ef7dad)

The first pass paved the whole clearing in pale beige flagstones that swallowed the dark figures and props, in 4 m blocks of differently angled stones. Now: grey-green stone (albedo about 0.8 x the old, tile noise halved), irregular stone islands in moss, earth and leaf litter, patches of different size with interlocking wavy borders, flat roots creeping in, and the floor palette moved from ochre toward olive and moss. `measure_paving_root.py` measures the exported glb and a render of the default frame (FOV 40, pitch 45, the r001 figures at 180 cm, Blender stand-in lighting; the figure silhouette comes from a figures-only render):

| Measure | Before (5ef7dad) | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paving luminance, mean (linear texel x vertex light, stone tops in the footprint) | 0.274 | **0.199 (-27 %)** |
| Paving luminance spread (std), std / mean | 0.059, 0.216 | **0.039 (-35 %)**, 0.194 |
| Paving luminance p5 / p95 | 0.184 / 0.383 | 0.146 / 0.264 |
| Stone tops, share of the 27 x 18 m footprint | about 75 % (non-stone 24.8 %) | 53 % (joints, earth, moss, flat roots 47 %) |
| Ground luminance behind the figures (display) | 0.337 | 0.215 |
| Figure luminance (display, mean of their pixels) | 0.119 | 0.106 |
| Figure-to-ground contrast \|Lg - Lf\| / (Lg + Lf) | 0.477 | 0.341 |
| Figure pixels brighter than the ground behind them by 15 % | 6.8 % | 9.2 % |
| Ground busyness (std / mean of the board centre) | 0.388 | 0.407 |
| Lattice R2 / R4 / R6, share within 3 degrees of the cell axes | 0.027 / 0.277 / 0.019, 1.6 % | 0.035 / 0.167 / 0.029, 0.7 % |

The figures are dark (mean 0.106), so a lower ground value reduces their dark-on-light contrast while their bright trim and the fungus glow, which are now brighter than the ground, gain; the busyness metric did not improve because earth between stones adds patchiness at the board centre even though the value difference is small. Judge it in the frames; a rim or fill lift on the figures (#147, the character material) is the lever that is not in this slice.

## Known limits, to judge in Unreal

**Unreal verification (PR #176, 2026-11-10):** the kit is imported and placed. The headless import added exactly the 72 kit packages, the `world-root` replacement changed exactly four files, and a rerun changed nothing. Automation passed 231/231, and galleries `board-root-r002` and `board-root-pillars` were captured. LOD counts read back as planned. In game the stone does not compete with the figures, so the instance tint stays neutral. The coordinator's on-screen pass is pending. The notes below are the Blender-phase record.

- At the Blender phase, nothing was imported or rendered in Unreal. LOD screen sizes, whether `COLOR_0.a` is read, the cost of a 183k-triangle one-material world (21 MB glb; #145's is 130k / 10 MB) and the real material look are unverified. About 40 % of the triangles are tile-cut bark, so a larger bark tile or fewer roots would cut it.
- The world shell is 192 x 207 m (a bowl rising to 9 m at the ground edge, trunks to 110 m for the great tree); a dark underlay and fog are expected beyond it. The tall masses sit outside r = 56.5 m, so they are rarely in frame from the battle camera: the visible Root is mostly the clearing, its roots, stumps, arches and fungus.
- The 4 m patch borders of #145 are gone (wavy, interlocking, thinned seams); the stone islands are separated by earth, so at far zoom the clearing reads as islands rather than blocks. The approach (`PATCH_SIZE_U`, `EARTH_P`, `INTERLOCK_U`, `FEATHER_U/DROP` in `paving_root.py`) is reusable for the Vigil.
- Kit atlases are per asset and duplicated across glbs (r001 convention). Variants share textures, so they read as one family.
- Occluders are whole arches, with pivots at the world origin; dither fade is per piece. Only 31 of the 62k test rays touch them, so fade will be rare.
- Root boards place `Theme->Pillar/Wall/Stones` today, and Vigil and Root encounters share them; phase 2 must choose the Root variants by theme.
