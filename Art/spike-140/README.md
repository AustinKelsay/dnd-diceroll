Throwaway (branch `spike/140-look-dev`, never merged). ## Metric Vigil world

Convention as r001: metres, +X forward, Z up, glTF Y-up export, `COLOR_0` baked light. Source scene is 2 units per cell; r001 `SCALE` 0.55 gave 110 cm cells, here `SCALE = 0.75` gives **1 cell = 150 cm, 18 x 12 cells = 27 m x 18 m, centred on the origin** (verified: no architecture triangle above 10 cm inside the footprint).

- **Kept architecture.** Only triangles inside the footprint (+0.5 unit) are cleared: 1,442 tris (80 whole obstacle components, plus the cut part of a few). Everything else stays. Pieces that block any of 72 candidate camera positions (3 distances x 3 pitches 35/45/60 x 8 yaws) to any of 864 ground targets, or that rise near the board, go to the separate occluder mesh: 55 components, iterated until a fresh ray test finds nothing blocking in the world mesh (5 passes, 0 residual rays; terrain/risers count as ground and are not tested). Far parts of chained pieces stay in the world mesh.
- **Ground.** One continuous mesh: 1,209 Voronoi stones (jittered grid sites, 7.5 cm joints) over a soil/moss base. Equivalent diameter p5/p50/p95 = 47 / 63 / 69 cm; widest chord p50 = 79 cm (p95 89 cm, so the largest stones exceed the 75 cm target); top height +-2.7 cm; random tilt <= 2.8 cm per metre; not aligned to the 150 cm cells. Moss comes from a noise field (plus a rim boost) as a mossy stone variant, green vertex tint and moss base quads; bare soil patches replace a few stones. Chamfered tops and sunken joints are real geometry (stones 35,855 tris, base 1,976).
- **Lighting.** r001's baked light resampled onto the new vertices (its shadows of cleared obstacles are lifted to 75% of the median), times a new ray-cast AO term (10 cosine rays, 1.8 m) per unique vertex (49,507 samples), joints darkened per stone. Architecture within 14 units of the board is subdivided to <= 75 cm edges (median edge 47 cm, p90 68 cm). No new direct-light bake with shadows was done.
- **Glow mask.** `COLOR_0.a` = 1 on glass (rose window and lancets), flames and a new rune ring inlay, else 0 (308 tris world, 566 occluders). The glTF `COLOR_0` is VEC4 normalised uint16; confirmed by reading the accessor type.
- **Atlas.** 2048 x 2048 shared by both glbs (identical image, asserted), 18 regions, 27% filled: new procedural 256 px tiles (4 paving variants, moss, soil, rune; 256 texels/m) beside r001's textures at native size (their architecture texel density is unchanged).

## Rigged fighter pilot

`exports/SK_Hero_FighterSpike.glb`: **3,441 tris**, 2,567 vertices, 1 material, **1024 x 1024** atlas (procedural; blue steel with pale scratch lines, gold trim, crimson cape, kite shield, longsword, as the r001 fighter), 0.84 MB, **182.0 cm** to the horn tips (helm 174 cm), pivot between the feet, facing +X.

**25 bones**, parent order: `root`, `pelvis`, `spine1`, `spine2`, `chest`, `neck`, `head`, `clavicle.L/R`, `upperarm.L/R`, `forearm.L/R`, `hand.L/R`, `thigh.L/R`, `shin.L/R`, `foot.L/R`, `toe.L/R`, `weapon_socket` (child of `hand.R`), `shield_socket` (child of `hand.L`).

**Weighting: rigid per segment, with one blended ring at waist, neck, elbows and knees, and a smoothly weighted cape.** Plate armour is made of overlapping pieces (pauldrons, faulds, cops, greaves, helm), each 100% on one bone, which is how the r001 figures read (hard faceted plates) and it costs nothing in skinning; the single blended rings hide the joint crack. At most 2 influences per vertex. This is cheaper than smooth skinning but a robed body (the wizard, soft clothing) would need smooth weights.

**Actions** (30 fps, scripted keyframes in `build_rig_spike.py`, first key repeated as last for a seamless loop): `Idle` frames 0-60 (2.0 s: breathing, weight shift, shield arm braced), `Walk` frames 0-30 (1.0 s, in place, one full stride: 28 degree leg swing, knee flex to 56 degrees, pelvis bob and yaw, counter-swinging arms).

## What was validated

- Re-import in a fresh Blender process (`validate_rig.py`): 25 bones with the names above; actions `Idle` (0-60) and `Walk` (0-30), only after setting the scene to 30 fps first (the importer converts seconds to frames at the scene rate, 24 by default); 3,441 tris; 1 material; 1024 px image; 25 vertex groups.
- Deformation: mid-idle moves vertices up to 4.4 cm; mid-walk (frames 8 and 23) up to 42 to 49 cm (hands, sword, feet). First and last pose of both loops are identical (0.0 mm).
- Mid-walk frame rendered: `previews/rig_walk_mid.png` (also bind and mid-idle). Knees, hips and arms bend cleanly; the front fauld clips the lifted thigh a little.
- World: footprint clearance (0 triangles), occlusion re-test, atlas identity, COLOR_0 alpha.
- Git LFS: `.gitattributes` already tracks `*.glb`, `*.blend` and `*.png`; nothing is missing.

## Previews (`previews/`, 1920 x 1080 EEVEE)

`contact_sheet.png` combines: `A_fov50_pitch58_board`, `B_fov40_pitch45_board`, `C_fov40_pitch35_party`, `grid_off` / `grid_on` (4-cell Manhattan reach: 18% grid lines, 50% contour, path ribbon, destination brackets, 56 cm footprint ring), `filter_closest` / `filter_linear` (same close shot), the rig strip and `occluders_highlight`. Stand-ins are r001 heroes and foes scaled x1.565 (to ~180 cm); the rigged fighter plays Idle frame 12. The world is shown unlit (texture x baked colour, glow x1.3), figures lit by a key and a fill, blob shadows only. Notes: Blender's `Linear` with 16x anisotropic is not Unreal's trilinear + aniso, and Blender cannot show a sharp-texel anti-aliased filter, so the filtering pair only shows the two extremes (Closest keeps texel edges crisp, Linear blurs the rune glyphs and stone detail at the grazing angle).

## Open issues

- The outer terraces are still r001's square flagstones at about 100 texels/m, so the new irregular paving stops abruptly at the 0.75 m rim and the two meet at a riser; a real build would extend the paving or make a transition ring.
- r001 architecture textures are untouched (128 to 256 px tiles), so a 2048 atlas gives no extra wall detail yet; the atlas is 27% full.
- Subdivision doubles as the vertex-density answer but makes T-junctions and inflates the occluder mesh to 41.6k tris; per-piece density, a bake with real direct light and shadows, and merging the occluders into a few instanced pieces are left.
- Stones are individual geometry (36k tris, one draw call); a decision on a ground mesh versus a vertex-painted plane with a stone normal-less texture is open. Some stones are wider than 75 cm.
- No IK or foot locking (Idle slides mm); cape has no bones of its own (rigid chest/spine/pelvis blend); no LODs; no separate caster/foe bodies; the fighter is a single body type.
- Not verified in Unreal: skeletal import settings, bone axes, clip names and 30 fps, whether the engine reads the alpha of `COLOR_0`, and the actual frame time of any of these.
- The lighting A/B (CSM + locals), Lumen comparison, and the 150 vs 130 cm cell check from the ticket need the Unreal harness and are not part of this Blender half.
