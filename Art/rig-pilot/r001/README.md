# Rigged character pilot (#147), Blender half

Phase 1 of #147: one shared humanoid skeleton, two base bodies, three characters, nine clips, glb exports and checks. Everything is original and generated by script in Blender 5.2.3 LTS; no outside content, kits, paid or AI services. Unreal was not run: import and integration (`UDiceRollTheme` definitions, captures) belong to phase 2.

## Skeleton contract (all characters share it)

Metres, +X forward, +Y left, Z up, foot pivot at the origin (both soles at z = 0), 30 fps. Armature object `Skeleton_Humanoid`, 25 bones, names and hierarchy never change; every character exports the same armature so one Unreal skeleton asset serves all of them. `root` is never animated (clips are in place, root drift 0 mm); the pelvis carries all body motion.

```
root
  pelvis (z .95)
    spine1 > spine2 > chest
      neck > head
      clavicle.L/R > upperarm.L/R > forearm.L/R > hand.L/R
        hand.R > weapon_socket     hand.L > shield_socket
    thigh.L/R > shin.L/R > foot.L/R > toe.L/R
```

Rest pose: standing, arms hang 22 degrees out from the body, elbows and knees straight, feet flat pointing +X. Bone heads (m): pelvis z .95, spine1 1.02, spine2 1.16, chest 1.30, neck 1.46, head 1.54 (tail 1.76), shoulder (upperarm head) y +-.22 z 1.42, elbow 30 cm and wrist 57 cm along the arm, hip y +-.10 z .93, knee z .50, ankle z .09, toe tail x .24. The exact heads and tails are `build_joints()` in `scripts/rigkit.py`; bone roll is 0 (Blender default).

Sockets: `weapon_socket` (child of `hand.R`, origin at the right palm, tail pointing +X) and `shield_socket` (child of `hand.L`, origin at the left forearm outer side, tail pointing +Y). Held items are skinned rigidly to them. Clips may rotate `weapon_socket` as a grip adjustment (Cast, Ranged, Downed) the way an engine weapon bone is animated; anything attached there follows. Held items are authored where the hand is in Idle and mapped back to the bind pose, so the bind pose shows them rotated; the contact sheet shows bind and Idle.

Skin: at most 4 influences per vertex (the meshes use 3). Heavy body: rigid per plate, one blended ring at waist, chest, neck, elbows and knees. Robed body: smooth, with the robe skirt blending pelvis and both thighs towards the hem, sleeves blending along the arm, back drape smooth.

## Characters

| Character | Body | Height (excluding held items) | Tris LOD0 / LOD1 / LOD2 | Gear |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fighter `SK_Hero_Fighter` | heavy | 183.4 cm | 3566 / 1681 (47%) / 972 (27%) | kite shield, longsword, cape, tabard |
| Wizard `SK_Hero_Wizard` | robed | 182.9 cm | 3750 / 1759 (47%) / 823 (22%) | staff with crystal, hat, back drape, spellbook |
| Raider `SK_Foe_Raider` | heavy | 203.8 cm with antlers | 3428 / 1642 (48%) / 966 (28%) | antlered helm, spiked pauldrons, axe |

Each has one material on its own 1024x1024 atlas (13 tiles of 256 px: plate, trim, cloth, leather, blade, glow, rune, skin, bone, accent, dark, emblem, emblem2). Vertex colour `Color` (glTF `COLOR_0`) is RGBA: RGB is a soft height shade (0.80 to 1.0), alpha is the glow mask. Only runes, gems and crystals have alpha 1 (sword fuller, pommel and brow gems; the wizard's hem runes, hat gem, amulet, staff crystal; the raider's axe and chest runes). Palettes are brighter than r001 on purpose (gate 1 found dark figures vanish on dark ground): light steel with pale bands, cream tabard, crimson cape with a pale hem; indigo robe with white trim and a pale mask and beard; tan hide with bone-white spikes and antlers and a red cloth.

## LODs

LODs are separate skinned meshes built by the same recipe at lower ring and side counts with small details dropped (not a decimation). LOD0 is `<name>.glb` with the clips; `<name>_LOD1.glb` and `<name>_LOD2.glb` carry the same 25-bone armature and no animation, to be imported as LOD meshes of the one skeletal mesh.

## Clips (30 fps, frame 0 start, in place)

Idle 60 f (2.0 s loop), Walk 30 f (1.0 s loop), Melee 30 f, Ranged 36 f, Cast 42 f, Hit 15 f, Downed 72 f (collapse to a kneeling slump by f40, held to f72), Death 78 f (falls on its back, held from f54), Recover 66 f (starts at the Downed hold pose, ends at the Idle pose). Death and Recover are two clips, so there are nine in total. Clips are sampled on every frame; non-loop clips start and end on Idle frame 0, Idle and Walk repeat their first pose on the last frame. Pelvis height is solved per frame against the floor from bone contact points (`ground_offset`), so feet, knees and hands do not sink. All clips are shared across characters; the wizard reads Cast with the staff swung forward and the fighter reads Melee with the sword chop. Ranged is a bow-draw and release pose.

## Scripts (repository root, one Blender process at a time)

| Script | Does |
| --- | --- |
| `blender -b --python Art/rig-pilot/r001/scripts/build.py [-- fighter wizard raider]` | builds meshes, atlas, weights, clips; exports glbs and `*_source.blend` into `Art/<asset-id>/r001/`; writes `metrics/build_*.json` |
| `blender -b --python Art/rig-pilot/r001/scripts/review.py -- validate <fighter\|wizard\|raider>` | fresh process: re-imports the glbs and writes `metrics/validate_*.json` |
| `blender -b --python Art/rig-pilot/r001/scripts/review.py -- sheet <bind\|clips\|stone>` | renders `previews/sheet_*.png` (needs ImageMagick 7) |
| `python3 Art/rig-pilot/r001/scripts/make_records.py` | writes the three `Art/<asset-id>/r001/asset.json` records |

`rigkit.py` (skeleton, atlas, primitives, FK, ground contact, export), `clips.py` (poses) and `characters.py` (recipes) are libraries.

## Checks and limits

Re-import in a fresh Blender: 25 bones, all nine clips with the expected ranges, Idle and Walk seam distance 0.000 mm (maximum vertex), LOD bone names identical, one material, 1024x1024 atlas, glow alpha present, at most 3 influences. Not done: any Unreal import, in-engine capture, retargeting, or playback convergence tests (phase 2). Known roughness: Death and Recover reuse one pose set for all characters, so the wizard's staff and robe clip through the body in Recover; held items are not collision-aware (the sword blade touches the floor in Downed); the plume, cape and hat are rigid or lightly skinned, with no cloth simulation; the thin shield and blades are single-thickness plates.
