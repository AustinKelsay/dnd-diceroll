# Rigged cast, batch 2 (#150), Blender half

Batch 2 adds the **barbarian, monk, paladin and ranger** and the tenth shared clip, **Brace**. Everything is original and generated by script in Blender 5.2.3 LTS; no outside content. The skeleton contract is unchanged ([`../r001/README.md`](../r001/README.md): 25 bones, sockets, 30 fps, in place, 1024x1024 atlas of 13 tiles, RGBA vertex colour with the glow mask in alpha, at most 4 influences; these meshes use 3). The batch 1 notes are in [`README.md`](README.md).

**The batch 1 files are not edited** (`cast_characters.py`, `build.py`, `review.py`, `make_records.py`, `README.md`), because the immutable r001 asset records of the rogue and cleric hash them. Batch 2 sits beside them: `cast_batch2.py` (recipes, atlases, painters; imports the batch 1 helpers `agile_body`, `cloth_sheet`, `arc_loft`, `dagger`, `pauldron_round`), `brace.py`, `build2.py`, `review2.py`, `make_records2.py`, `compare_r002.py`.

## Decisions

| Question | Decision | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Barbarian body | The heavy body's skeleton fit with its own bulkier recipe (`barbarian_body`, girth x1.09): painted bare torso and arms, fur kilt, wrapped leather legs. Not the heavy plate recipe | The brief asks for a bulkier variant inside the height band (177.1 cm). Girth by proportions keeps the skin weights of the heavy body's joints valid |
| Barbarian read against the raider | Tawny fur mantle with dark tips, bare skin with white stripes and woad-blue bands, bronze armlets, braided auburn hair and beard, a headband, a double-bit crescent greataxe with **no spikes or antlers**, ember runes only on the blades | The raider is tan hide with bone spikes, antlers, a skull helm and red cloth. The barbarian has no horn or spike anywhere and shows skin and a face. The sheet `sheet_14` puts both side by side on dark stone |
| Greataxe grip | In the **right hand only**, on a long haft | In the shared Idle the two hands are 0.84 m apart (arms hang out at 22 degrees), so a straight haft cannot pass both fists. A second hand on the haft would need per-character clips, which batch 1 ruled out. The left hand is free; the heavy chop of Melee still reads |
| Monk | Agile body, saffron gi and sleeveless ochre short tabard with dark trim, a wide rust sash with two tails, cream wraps over the hands and forearms, dark wrap rings, bead necklace with one glowing bead, headband with knot tails and a topknot, a **bo staff slung on the back**. **Unarmed in hand** | The Melee clip is a chop and the monk's catalog abilities are palm and fist strikes. The slung staff gives a silhouette without a held weapon |
| Paladin | Heavy body in polished silver plate (round pauldrons, gold trim), a closed helm with a purple crest fin and a glowing brow gem, a heraldic purple tabard with an original crown device, a white cape lined in royal purple, a **warhammer** (square head, back beak) and a **tall tower shield** | The fighter has blue steel, a crimson cape, a longsword and a kite shield; the cleric has ivory, teal, gold and a round shield and flanged mace. White, silver and purple, a tall rectangular shield and a hammer share none of that. The device is a three-pointed crown (no real-world heraldry) |
| Ranger | Agile body in olive-tan leather, a **pointed hood with a drooping tail** and a cream feather, a moss-green mantle and a **short cape to the hip** with a russet hem and dark-green lining, a quiver with fletched arrows on the back, a short blade on `weapon_socket` and a **longbow on `shield_socket`** | The rogue is a pale blue-green long cloak with a cream hem and lining, a rounded hood and twin daggers. The ranger is yellower and more saturated, with a shorter cape, a pointed drooping hood, and the tall bow, which is the biggest silhouette change |
| Longbow | Authored where the left hand is in the **Ranged hold** (frame 22), not Idle | With the shared Ranged clip the left arm points at the target, the right hand comes back to the cheek. A bow authored at Idle would be tilted about 36 degrees when drawn. Authored at the hold, the bow is upright when drawn and tilted (about 30 degrees, held low) in Idle. The string is a straight line; the right hand does not hold it |
| Short blade and Ranged | The ranger carries the short blade in the right hand all the time, so in Ranged the blade is raised beside the head, the same as the rogue's dagger | Melee must read as a blade attack; a sheathed blade would make the chop an empty hand. The draw pose still reads from the bow, the pointing arm and the stance |
| Hands and held items | Clips stay shared; nothing needed a per-character fix | As batch 1 |

## Brace (the tenth clip)

`brace.py` registers `Brace` in the r001 clip table without editing `r001/scripts/clips.py`, so the r001 build still makes nine clips and every batch 2 build, validation and sheet sees ten. 24 frames (0.8 s at 30 fps), not a loop, starts and ends on Idle frame 0, in place (root drift 0 mm). A short coil (knees give, the weapon arm drops back), the weapon arm snaps up and forward above the head with the chest open and the head lifted, a short hold, and back to Idle. The free fist closes at the chest; a held shield or bow rises with the left arm. The other nine clips are untouched.

**All nine rigs have it.** `build2.py --r002` re-exports fighter, wizard, raider, rogue and cleric as **revision 2** (records are immutable per revision) into `Art/<asset-id>/r002/`: the LOD0 glb with ten clips and a new `*_source.blend`; the LOD1 and LOD2 glbs carry no clips and are unchanged, so r002 records reference r001's. `compare_r002.py` re-imports each old and new LOD0 glb in a fresh process and proves: the same vertices (0.000 mm maximum difference), the same bones, the nine old clips with identical frame ranges and identical keyframes on all 2,250 fcurves (0 difference), and exactly one extra clip, Brace (`metrics/compare_r002.json`).

One material per character on its own 1024x1024 atlas (the same 13 tiles). Atlas marks painted over the base tiles: the barbarian's fur (`cloth`) and warpaint (`emblem`), the paladin's crown device (`emblem` tabard, `emblem2` shield). Glow alpha only on runes and gems: barbarian axe runes (24 vertices), monk bead (36), paladin hammer, helm, shield and pommel gems and runes (120), ranger blade gem and rune, bow riser gem (84). 3 influences at most. Heights are 175 to 185 cm; the LOD1 of the barbarian is 44% rather than 50%.

## Scripts (repository root, one Blender process at a time, under the machine lock)

| Script | Does |
| --- | --- |
| `blender -b --python Art/rig-pilot/cast/scripts/build2.py [-- barbarian monk paladin ranger]` | builds meshes, atlas, weights, ten clips; exports glbs and `*_source.blend` into `Art/<asset-id>/r001/`; writes `metrics/build_*.json` |
| `... build2.py -- --r002 fighter wizard raider rogue cleric` | re-exports the five earlier characters with ten clips into `Art/<asset-id>/r002/` |
| `... review2.py -- validate <key> [--r002]` | fresh process: re-imports the glbs and writes `metrics/validate_<key>[_r002].json` (bones, ten clips, seams, LOD bone names, influences, glow alpha, root drift) |
| `... compare_r002.py -- <dir with the old glbs> fighter wizard raider rogue cleric` | proves the re-exports changed nothing but the extra clip |
| `... review2.py -- sheet2 bind\|turn\|clips\|brace\|stone` | renders `previews/sheet_06..14` (ImageMagick 7) |
| `python3 Art/rig-pilot/cast/scripts/make_records2.py` | writes the four r001 records and the five r002 records |

## Known roughness

Clips are shared: the greataxe and warhammer are one-handed holds with the other hand free; the ranger's blade rises in Ranged, and the string is not drawn; the paladin's tall shield swings high in Cast and Brace; the barbarian's fur kilt and the paladin's tabard and cape are lightly skinned sheets with no cloth simulation; Downed and Recover lower long weapons close to the floor without collision awareness (the axe and hammer heads touch the ground, the bow clips in Death). The monk's slung staff clips the shoulder blade in Downed.
