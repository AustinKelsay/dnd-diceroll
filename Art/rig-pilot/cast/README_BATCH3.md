# Rigged cast, batch 3 (#150), Blender half

Batch 3 adds the **sorcerer, warlock and druid**. Everything is original and generated by script in Blender 5.2.3 LTS; no outside content. The skeleton contract is unchanged ([`../r001/README.md`](../r001/README.md): 25 bones, sockets, 30 fps, in place, 1024x1024 atlas of 13 tiles, RGBA vertex colour with the glow mask in alpha, at most 4 influences; these meshes use 3) and the ten shared clips (Brace from batch 2) are used as they are: no per-class clip was needed. Earlier notes: [`README.md`](README.md) (batch 1), [`README_BATCH2.md`](README_BATCH2.md).

**No batch 1 or batch 2 file is edited**, because their immutable asset records hash them. Batch 3 sits beside them: `cast_batch3.py` (recipes, atlases, painters; imports `cast_batch2.py` for the Brace clip and the batch 2 helpers), `build3.py`, `review3.py`, `make_records3.py`.

## Decisions

| Question | Decision | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sorcerer body | The **agile body** under a **fitted crimson coat** that is open at the front and flares to the knee (`arc_loft`, black lining, ember-gold edging), black trousers and boots, a short black-violet mantle with an ember hem, deeper on the left shoulder | The lead allowed a shorter, more fitted robe or a mantle over a tunic. The visible trousers and boots, the open coat and the short mantle give a silhouette that does not overlap the wizard's floor-length indigo robe with a pointed hat |
| Sorcerer magic | Bare head with swept-back black hair, a crimson flame collar behind the head (flat crimson licks, no horn or spike), thin glowing **ember veins** on both sleeves, and a **crackling crystal** in the right hand: a gold claw cradle, a tall crystal, two small tilted ones and jagged sparks. No staff | Innate magic, shown on the body and in one hand. Veins, crystal, sparks and the brow, buckle and vial gems are the only glow vertices (482 at LOD0) |
| Warlock body | The **agile body** under a long hooded **plum coat** (two front flaps, a back sheet with a jagged pale-lavender hem and green line), a pale bone pauldron of stacked plates on the left shoulder only, a plain plum mantle on the right, a tall pointed cowl lined in pale bone cloth | The coat length and the asymmetry keep it apart from the rogue's sage cloak. The pale lining, pauldron and hem band are the batch 1 readability approach for a dark palette (the plum is lifted to a mid-dark value, not near-black) |
| Warlock pact | A **bone-chain bandolier** from the right shoulder to an **eye-sigil tome** hanging at the left hip (the eye is an original almond-and-slit sigil drawn by `paint_eye`, with a glowing rune on the cover), a **pendant** with a glowing green eye on the chest, a green glowing sigil on the back of the left hand and glyphs on the coat hem; a **rod** in the right hand (dark shaft, pale rings, a three-claw cradle and a green orb). Sickly green glow only | Matches the lead's pact token, glow on hands and sigils and rod. The half mask first tried over the mouth read as a skull and was removed; the face is an ashen grey-green |
| Druid body | The **robed body**: a bark-brown tunic to the shin under a **split moss-green robe** with a ragged leaf hem, an amber-banded rope belt, a herb pouch, **three tiers of leaves** as a mantle, a leaf cape down the back, wooden bracers, wrapped boots | Robe and layered mantle against the ranger's tunic, short cape and hood. Value is the other split: the druid is a bright moss green over dark bark brown, the ranger an olive-tan with a dark green cape |
| Druid circlet | A thin twig ring with a ring of small leaves and one amber gem, **no vertical sprigs**. Long brown hair, tan skin | The first version had two short sprigs that read as small horns in the turnaround; they were removed so the head cannot read as the antlered raider foe |
| Druid staff | A **gnarled** wooden staff taller than the head: a bent path with knots, a crook at the top cradling a glowing **amber seed**, a side twig with two leaves, amber bindings | The wizard's staff is straight with a pale crystal; the crook, the knots and the amber make the druid's read as a branch |
| Palettes (contrast gate) | The warlock's plum and its dark parts, and the sorcerer's black-violet mantle, trousers and boots, were lifted after the in-game contrast measurement (the first warlock palette's worst default-zoom shot was 1.25 against a gate of 1.4, the sorcerer's mean 2.35 against 2.3). The warlock is a bright violet-plum with a pale lavender hem, lining and trim, not near-black | The rigged Rim 1.2 / Fill 1.0 do not lift a dark palette; readability on dark ground came from value, as for the rogue in batch 1. The measured numbers are in `docs/assets.md` |
| Clips | **All ten shared clips, no override** | The crystal, the rod and the staff were checked on the Melee, Ranged, Cast, Downed, Death, Recover and Brace sheets (19 to 21). The crystal and rod rise with the arm in Cast and Brace; the staff swings as the wizard's does. The Ranged clip raises the right hand with the focus, as it does for the wizard |

## Scripts (repository root, one Blender process at a time, under the machine lock, always with `--python-exit-code 1`)

Blender exits 0 when a script raises unless it is started with `--python-exit-code 1`; always pass it.

| Script | Does |
| --- | --- |
| `blender -b --python-exit-code 1 --python Art/rig-pilot/cast/scripts/build3.py [-- sorcerer warlock druid]` | builds meshes, atlas, weights, ten clips; exports glbs and `*_source.blend` into `Art/<asset-id>/r001/`; writes `metrics/build_*.json` |
| `... review3.py -- validate <key>` | fresh process: re-imports the glbs and writes `metrics/validate_<key>.json` with a `checks` table and `pass`; **any failed check exits non-zero** (25 bones matching the contract, ten clips with the expected ranges, one material and one 1024 atlas, glow alpha, at most 4 influences, 175 to 185 cm, 3 to 6k tris, LOD1 40 to 55% and LOD2 20 to 32%, LOD bone names, loop seams and every action clip ending on Idle at 0.01 mm, root drift 0.01 mm; this is review2.py's gate) |
| `... review3.py -- sheet3 bind\|turn\|clips\|stone` | renders `previews/sheet_17..22` (ImageMagick 7) |
| `python3 Art/rig-pilot/cast/scripts/make_records3.py` | writes the three `Art/<asset-id>/r001/asset.json` records; **refuses** (exit 1, nothing written) unless every validation JSON passes |

## Known roughness

Clips are shared. The sorcerer's Ranged raises the crystal overhead (a throw or a cast, not a bow draw), as the rogue's dagger does; in play the three casters' ranged basic attack plays Cast (a spell bolt from the focus), so that Ranged clip is not used for them. The warlock's rod and the druid's staff clip the body in Downed and Recover (no collision awareness), as the wizard's staff does; the druid's long hair and leaf mantle and the warlock's long coat are lightly skinned sheets with no cloth simulation. The warlock's coat panels show vertical fold stripes from the shared fold tile. The sorcerer's glowing veins are thin boxes over the sleeves, not a texture.
