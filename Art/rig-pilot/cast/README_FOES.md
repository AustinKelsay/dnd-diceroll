# Rigged cast, foe batch (#150), Blender half

The foe batch adds the **scout, archer, skirmisher, brute and warden** on the shared skeleton. The scout and archer replace the static `foe-scout` and `foe-archer` meshes; the skirmisher, brute and warden are the new foe roles of the encounter-identity contract (#188, `docs/runs/gameplay-depth/11-encounter-identity-contract.md` on `feature/188-encounter-identity`) and appear in play only once #188 lands. Everything is original and generated by script in Blender 5.2.3 LTS; no outside content. The skeleton contract is unchanged ([`../r001/README.md`](../r001/README.md): 25 bones, sockets, 30 fps, in place, 1024x1024 atlas of 13 tiles, RGBA vertex colour with the glow mask in alpha, at most 4 influences; these meshes use 3) and the ten shared clips (Brace from batch 2). Earlier notes: [`README.md`](README.md), [`README_BATCH2.md`](README_BATCH2.md), [`README_BATCH3.md`](README_BATCH3.md).

**No earlier batch file is edited** (their immutable asset records hash them). The foe batch sits beside them: `cast_foes.py` (recipes, atlases, painters; imports `cast_batch2.py` and `cast_characters.py` helpers), `overrides_foes.py`, `build_foes.py`, `review_foes.py`, `make_records_foes.py`. Preview sheets are named `sheet_f01`..`sheet_f12`, so they cannot collide with the numbered batch 4 sheets.

## Decisions

| Question | Decision | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hostile family | The raider's palette: **tan hide, red cloth and bone-white**, ember-orange trim and glow, a dark brown for wraps and hair. Every foe except the warden is built from it; the warden is the Vault's own, cold pale stone and verdigris with the Vault's cyan glow (0.5, 0.85, 1.0, the Waystone's, #197) | A foe must read as an enemy at a glance and never as a hero class. No hero uses red cloth with bone-white; the sheets put each foe beside the hero it is nearest to (`sheet_f09`, `sheet_f10`) |
| Scout (agile) | Tan hide cowl lined in red, a **red scarf over the lower face**, hide mantle with a bone-trimmed edge and a short pelt, bone charms on a red cross strap and at the cowl, red sash and loincloth, one bone spike on the left spaulder, a **short leaf-bladed spear** with red tassels (a short spear, so the silhouette is not the raider's axe) | The scout is the raider's light kin; the cowl, scarf and spear separate it from the rogue's hood and daggers |
| Archer (agile) | Hide hood lined in red with a **bone-white skull-like half mask**, bone-fletched red-flighted arrows in a quiver, a hunting knife on `weapon_socket` (hidden during the draw), a **shortbow** on `shield_socket` authored at the hold of its own `Ranged_Archer` clip | The bone mask is the silhouette that tells it from the ranger's green pointed hood and longbow. "Vault bow" (the finale archer) uses this rig |
| Skirmisher (agile, #188: fast, low HP) | The leanest foe: **bare skin painted with a red diagonal war stripe and bone dashes**, a hide cross harness, a bone-charm necklace, a feather crest and feather clusters on the shoulders (bone-white and red), two long red streamers and headband tails, **twin sickles** (right on `weapon_socket`, left on `shield_socket`) | A running-light silhouette: no cloak, no hood, bare arms, trailing red. The curved twin blades read at the default zoom where the rogue's straight daggers do not |
| Brute (heavy, #188: slow, heavy, "Raider brute") | The barbarian's bulked body recipe at girth 1.10, but hostile: bare painted skin with war stripes, a **huge spiked hide pauldron** on the left (a small two-spike one on the right), a ragged red kilt and cape, the raider's dark helm with a bone brow, nasal bar and tusks and **bull horns**, a two-handed **stone maul** on `weapon_socket` | The raider's family (hide, bone, spikes, red) in the barbarian's mass, with horns where the raider has antlers and no face showing. The barbarian has a bare face, braids, fur and no spike or horn |
| Warden (heavy, #188: finale anchor, "Vault warden") | Pale cold stone plate with **verdigris trim**, massive stacked pauldrons with three raised fins each, a Vault-teal tabard and cape with a sigil, a closed great helm whose **face plate carries a T-shaped cyan glowing mask** (eye slit and nasal bar, glowing teeth), a verdigris **crown and a tall crest**, a **tower shield** (the Vault sigil, cyan boss) and a **halberd** with cyan runes | The boss figure: the biggest silhouette (crest, fins, shield and a 1.8 m halberd) and the Vault's own colours, so it cannot be mistaken for the paladin's white and purple or the fighter's blue steel |
| Per-class overrides | `Ranged_Archer` (36 frames) and `Melee_Brute` (30 frames), `overrides_foes.py`: the **same two override motions as the ranger's bow draw and the barbarian's two-handed swing**, authored through `overrides.py`'s solver (not edited). The archer's draw has the ranger's timing, so its release frame is the ranger's and the lightless arrow (#198) leaves at it; the brute's maul is authored on the barbarian's 60 degree haft axis | Real bow draw and a real two-handed swing, at no new timing contract. Both start and end on Idle frame 0 and are in place; the validator checks it |
| Clips | Ten shared clips for all five; the brute and the archer also carry their one override | As batch 2 |
| Height bands | Scout 181.7 cm, archer 184.2 cm, skirmisher 182.6 cm (all 175 to 185); brute **204.6 cm** (the horn tips; the raider with antlers is 203.8); warden **204.6 cm** (the crest, below the 210 cm ceiling). Heights exclude held items | Lead calls |

## Scripts (repository root, one Blender process at a time, under the machine lock, always with `--python-exit-code 1`)

| Script | Does |
| --- | --- |
| `blender -b --python-exit-code 1 --python Art/rig-pilot/cast/scripts/build_foes.py [-- scout archer skirmisher brute warden]` | builds meshes, atlas, weights, ten clips and the override clip; exports glbs and `*_source.blend` into `Art/<asset-id>/r001/`; writes `metrics/build_*.json` |
| `blender -b --python-exit-code 1 --python Art/rig-pilot/cast/scripts/review_foes.py -- validate <key>` | fresh process: re-imports the glbs and writes `metrics/validate_<key>.json` with a `checks` table and `pass`; **any failed check exits non-zero** (25 bones, ten clips, one material and one 1024 atlas, glow alpha, at most 4 influences, the foe's height band, 3 to 6k tris, LOD1 40 to 55% and LOD2 20 to 32%, LOD bone names, seams and Idle-to-Idle one-shots at 0.01 mm, root drift 0.01 mm, the override clip's range, Idle ends and root drift, and the archer's knife hidden from frame 2 to 31) |
| `blender -b --python-exit-code 1 --python Art/rig-pilot/cast/scripts/review_foes.py -- sheetf bind\|turn\|clips\|stone\|override` | renders `previews/sheet_f01..f12` (ImageMagick 7) |
| `python3 Art/rig-pilot/cast/scripts/make_records_foes.py` | writes the five `Art/<asset-id>/r001/asset.json` records; **refuses** (exit 1, nothing written) unless every validation JSON passes |

## Known roughness

Clips are shared except the two overrides. The spear, sickles and halberd are carried as the shared Idle carries any right-hand weapon (raised, near the shoulder); the halberd and the brute's maul clip the body in Downed and Recover (no collision awareness), as the paladin's hammer and the barbarian's axe do; the brute's maul is carried one-handed in the shared clips and two-handed in `Melee_Brute` (its solver residual is the barbarian's, 33.6 cm at worst at the coil key). Hoods, capes, streamers and the warden's tabard are lightly skinned sheets with no cloth simulation. The warden's glowing mask is a flat T-shaped plate, not a face.
