# Rigged cast, batch 1 (#150), Blender half

The full rigged cast comes in batches (parent #138, owner gate after each). Batch 1 finishes the original four classes: the **rogue** and the **cleric**, plus the pieces later batches reuse (the agile body, the cast recipe layout, the review and record scripts). Everything is original and generated by script in Blender 5.2.3 LTS; no outside content, kits, paid or AI services.

**The skeleton contract is unchanged** and lives in [`../r001/README.md`](../r001/README.md): the shared 25-bone armature `Skeleton_Humanoid` (names, hierarchy, rest pose, sockets, metres, +X forward, 30 fps), the 1024x1024 atlas of 13 tiles, RGBA vertex colour with the glow mask in alpha, at most 4 influences, nine clips, in place. These scripts import `rigkit.py`, `clips.py` and the r001 helpers from `../r001/scripts` and never copy or edit them, so r001 stays reproducible. Only the recipes (`scripts/cast_characters.py`) are new.

## Decisions

| Question | Decision | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rogue body | New **agile body** (`agile_body`): slim torso, arms and legs at about 80% of the heavy body's girth, smooth skin over every joint, no plates; neck, head, cloak and gear belong to the character | The brief asks for a lighter, leather body that reads slimmer than the heavy one. Later agile classes (ranger, monk, bard) call the same function with their own regions |
| Cleric body | **Robed** (cassock to mid-shin, smooth skirt weights as the wizard's) under a rigid steel cuirass, gold gorget and round pauldrons, a teal tabard and a pale back mantle | A heavy-bodied cleric would be a recolour of the fighter at the 90 px default zoom. The robe gives a different silhouette; the ivory, teal and gold palette, the coif and circlet, the round shield and the mace separate it from the indigo wizard. Skirt skinning is already validated on all nine clips |
| Clips | **All nine shared clips, no per-character clips** | Daggers (rogue) and mace and round shield (cleric) were checked on the Melee, Ranged, Cast, Downed, Death and Recover sheets and do not pass through the body; the blades are short and the grip adjustments of `weapon_socket` suit them. Per-character clips would need new `A_*` assets and per-character animation sets for a cosmetic gain. Known roughness is listed below |
| Rogue palette (readability, #148 finding) | Sage-green cloak with a cream hem and a **pale hood lining**, cream trim at the collar, bracers, boot cuffs and spaulders, a pale scarf and mask, a bright **copper sash** with a long tail, tan studded leather, steel daggers with mint gem and rune glow | The dark rogue merged with shadow pools at the default zoom. Pale trim, a light hood lining and a sash keep a silhouette on dark ground; the character material also takes Rim 1.6 and Fill 1.25 in Unreal. The measured result is in `docs/assets.md` |
| Cleric symbol | An original **dawn** emblem: a half sun over a horizon bar with short rays, on the shield and the tabard (drawn by `paint_dawn`) | No real-world religious symbol (the #147 emblem review); the fighter has a sunburst, so the cleric's is a rising half sun |

## Scripts (repository root, one Blender process at a time)

| Script | Does |
| --- | --- |
| `blender -b --python Art/rig-pilot/cast/scripts/build.py [-- rogue cleric]` | builds meshes, atlas, weights, the nine clips; exports glbs and `*_source.blend` into `Art/<asset-id>/r001/`; writes `metrics/build_*.json` |
| `blender -b --python Art/rig-pilot/cast/scripts/review.py -- validate <rogue\|cleric>` | fresh process: re-imports the glbs and writes `metrics/validate_*.json` (bones, clips, seams, LOD bone names, influences, glow alpha, root drift) |
| `blender -b --python Art/rig-pilot/cast/scripts/review.py -- sheet <bind\|turn\|clips\|stone>` | renders `previews/sheet_*.png` (needs ImageMagick 7) |
| `python3 Art/rig-pilot/cast/scripts/make_records.py` | writes the two `Art/<asset-id>/r001/asset.json` records |

A later batch adds its characters to `CHARACTERS` and `ASSET_IDS` in `cast_characters.py` (or a new recipe module beside it) and to `RIG` in `Content/Python/import_dark_fantasy_theme.py`.

## Checks and limits

Re-import in a fresh Blender: 25 bones matching the contract, all nine clips with the expected ranges, Idle and Walk seams 0.000 mm (maximum vertex), LOD bone names identical, one material, 1024x1024 atlas, glow alpha present, at most 3 influences, root drift 0 mm in every clip. Preview sheets: bind and Idle beside the fighter and wizard, a turnaround of both, all nine clips at three frames each, and the party on dark stone at the gate-1 camera.

Known roughness: the clips are the shared pilot clips, so the rogue's Ranged clip raises the right dagger straight up (it reads as a throw or a signal, not a bow draw) and the cleric's mace and shield pose as the fighter's sword and shield do; the cleric's robe folds oddly in Downed and Recover, as the wizard's does; Downed and Recover lower the daggers close to the floor without collision awareness; the hood, cloak and mantle are lightly skinned sheets with no cloth simulation; the shield and spaulders are single shells. The rogue's non-spell Protect and Healing specials play the shared Cast clip (an arms-raised gesture) because the pose mapping is by effect (see `docs/assets.md`).
