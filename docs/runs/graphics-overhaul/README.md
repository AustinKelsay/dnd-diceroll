  - **Gate 2 (#148), default framing, from the on-screen data (2026-11-10):** the default zoom targets figure size, not the whole board. On the 16×11, 17×12 and 18×12 boards the whole-board fit left figures about 50 px tall at 1920×1080 and about 30 px at 1280×720. The default is now the closer of the whole-board fit and the distance at which a standing 180 cm figure at the focus is 88 px tall at 1080 viewport pixels (a fixed fraction of the viewport height, so about 59 px at 720), on the same zoom-tied pitch curve. Boards that fit at the closer distance keep their fit. Wheel-out and the `far` pose still reach the whole-board fit (`FarStop` and the far pose are unchanged), so "see everything" stays one zoom away. Where the whole board does not fit, the view centres on the active unit and keeps it inside the HUD-safe rect every frame. The pan bound is tighter: the view may show at most 1.5 cells past the board's outer tile edge, an axis the view is wider than stays centred on the board, and nothing pans while the whole board is in view (it was 2 cells past the outer cell centres for the focus alone). See `docs/architecture.md` (battle camera).
  - **#148 readability polish (lead, 2026-11-10):** from the on-screen passes (the dark rogue and raiders merge with shadow pools at the default zoom; the r001 braziers are sparse on the larger Root clearing). Every character material goes to `Rim` 1.6 and `Fill` 1.25 (was 1 and 1; the ten `MI_*` listed in [assets](../../assets.md#character-readability-issue-148)) and the ground is left alone, as the figure-contrast addendum on #149 says. Braziers move from seven fixed spots to a perimeter ring, one every four cells, 1.25 cells outside the playable edge, jittered by the board seed (12 on 16 x 11, 14 on 18 x 12, on every world); banners stay; the ring shares the old total light (280 cd) and keeps at most two shadowed local lights ([architecture](../../architecture.md#battle-camera-and-lighting-143)). Presentation only.

## Remaining limits (2026-11-12)

What the overhaul does not establish, for the lead and Austin. The release notes repeat the ones that matter to a player.

- **Mac #32 and #35.** [#32](https://github.com/AustinKelsay/dnd-diceroll/issues/32) (a one-time rendering assertion in scene culling, accepted risk) and [#35](https://github.com/AustinKelsay/dnd-diceroll/issues/35) (an intermittent packaged startup crash in the LLM tracker, 1 in 12 on macOS) are open and unresolved; their recurrences are reported, not fixed. Linux results do not resolve them, and nothing here was run on a Mac. Instancing and the choice of no Lumen, virtual shadow maps or Nanite were meant to reduce the risk, not eliminate it.
- **Linux shutdown assertion after failed tests, [#157](https://github.com/AustinKelsay/dnd-diceroll/issues/157).** A failing automation run can crash at renderer shutdown in virtual-texture cleanup (it happened twice on #205's early runs, after tests had already failed). The helper now reports a failed lifecycle even when tests also fail (#161). It has not recurred on any run whose tests passed, including this acceptance.
- **Gallery shader crash [#174](https://github.com/AustinKelsay/dnd-diceroll/issues/174), mitigated.** Capture mode drains shader and asset compilation on its first frame and around Resume (#178). That reduces the offscreen "null shader binding in mesh draw" crash and does not prove it gone. A separate startup quirk, an SDL SIGFPE in `handle_wl_output_done` on about 1 in 10 launches on a headless Wayland output, is retried once by `perf` and by hand elsewhere.
- **Physical pointer and pan/zoom coverage.** Every on-screen pass used virtual keyboard and pointer input (`wtype`, a `zwlr_virtual_pointer` helper) on a nested headless compositor. A real mouse wheel, middle-drag orbit and physical pointer motion, and pan/zoom timing with a person, are unverified. Capture and perf runs bind no input.
- **Human balance and fun.** No human has played the new look for balance, fun or readability over a whole run. Compact balance is #112's, and the encounter tuning waits for #188.
- **Rogues from behind.** Four rogues are told apart from the front and three-quarter views by their sashes, but from behind and side-on, and in the game's home yaw (-90, where the party lines up behind each other) they are still hard to tell apart. The only large surface seen there is the cloak, the `cloth` base tile with its hem painted inside the same tile, so a hem band needs a tile split (a repaint of `T_SK_Hero_Rogue` and new UVs for the hem faces). Deferred by the lead; paladins and fighters read from every angle.
- **Skirmisher, brute and warden not in play.** They exist as rigs, in `FoeRigs`, in a name-to-role table and in capture fixtures, and appear in a real run only after [#188](https://github.com/AustinKelsay/dnd-diceroll/issues/188) (encounter identity) lands. The brute (about 194 cm in game) is barely taller than the party's tallest at default zoom, and the warden's pale stone sits near the cleric's and paladin's values (it reads as a foe by its silhouette, red bar and Vault-cyan mask, which resolves only at 2x zoom). Their `HeightScale` and palette are tuned against real encounters then; the name table can go when #188's saved `Role` is reliable. No encounter has more than three foes, so a five-look fight was measured only as two three-foe fixtures.
- **HUD [#200](https://github.com/AustinKelsay/dnd-diceroll/issues/200).** On the route screen the hero-details panels for slots 2 to 4 draw under the route banner and the left card. It predates the overhaul's tabletop PR and is open.
- **Perf was measured on a headless nested output, not a physical display.** Passes ran at 1920x1080, `-novsync`, sound on, `--cores 2`, presented to a nested headless Hyprland. The GPU time is real and GPU timestamps are present, but the present path, a real display's compositor and a real refresh cycle are not represented. The numbers are for this GPU (RTX 4080 SUPER), driver and the machine's shared load.
- **Nanite and SM6 not certified ([#193](https://github.com/AustinKelsay/dnd-diceroll/issues/193) / [#105](https://github.com/AustinKelsay/dnd-diceroll/issues/105)).** The theme materials still lack the Nanite usage flag. Linux targets `VULKAN_SM5`, where Nanite is off, so nothing here exercises the SM6 path (Mac Metal warned in the past). Nanite is off on every imported mesh by decision, with explicit LODs.
- **Other limits the PRs recorded.**
  - The Vault's artifact-recovered moment was not seen on screen (the scripted player cannot win the finale); the single apse light is covered by tests. The default home view crops the Vault apse under the hint strip (the far view frames it), and party overlap at yaw 180 is inherent to that yaw.
  - The Vault occluder mesh has LOD1 only (pinned sharp-edge vertices stop a collapse at 54%).
  - The barbarian's one-handed carry in the shared clips and the paladin's one-handed hammer with a tall shield are accepted exceptions.
  - The wizard's Fire Bolt brightens its caster's box to 1.76% white for about 6 frames at release (under the 2% gate, existing behaviour); the Vault caster boxes at 1.4 to 2.2% white equal the no-light controls (scene content).
  - The rogue's single worst accent view is 24.1% of the figure (the class mean is 7.8%, the gate 10%); `accent_share.py` measures the middle half of each footprint box, so the paladin's shield and boots read 0 px from the front there although the sheets show them.
  - The four copies of a duplicate class can be as close as 10 dE (a paladin back view with a handful of pixels).
  - The scout, skirmisher, brute and warden Hit flinch and the animated brute fall were not examined live (convergence tests cover them); no pixel A/B of the default party against the pre-change build was taken for #205 (auto-exposure moves with the scene).
  - Per-PR on-screen passes at the merging head were not repeated on the final integrated head; this acceptance used the gallery, tests and the perf gate there.
  - Windows is still not a validated host ([#51](https://github.com/AustinKelsay/dnd-diceroll/issues/51)) and Mac is no longer one.
