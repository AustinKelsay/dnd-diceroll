## Graphics overhaul integrated (2026-11-12)

The graphics overhaul ([#138](https://github.com/AustinKelsay/dnd-diceroll/issues/138), run record [graphics-overhaul](graphics-overhaul/README.md), [release notes](graphics-overhaul/release-notes.md)) is merged to `staging` through #139 to #150 (head `f09abfe`, #205), with #151 (bigger arenas) closed as not planned and #152 (integrated acceptance, this record's PR) the last ticket. It changes presentation only: no rules, RNG, save schema or authoritative state (ADR-0001). What a new agent needs to know:

- **The look is data-driven and falls back.** One board metric (150 cm cells, 175 to 185 cm figures); `UDiceRollTheme` holds world kits (`EDiceRollKit { Vigil, Root, Vault }` with `KitFor`), the overview set (`LoadOverview()`, r002 or r001, never a mix), per-class and per-foe rigs (`ClassRigs`, `FoeRigs`, `RigForClass`, `RigForFoe`, each `FDiceRollCharacter` with a `HeightScale`) and the 70 subclass palettes as material-instance parameters (`ExpeditionPalette::ForUnit`, generated `ExpeditionPaletteTable.inl`; regenerate with `Art/rig-pilot/cast/scripts/palettes.py`, `--check` verifies it is current). Anything missing or failing to load falls back as a whole to the earlier kit, then to engine primitives; `-ExpeditionPrimitives` forces primitives and `--static-figures` renders the static r001 figures. Decoration uses its own stable hash, never combat or run RNG.
- **All 13 classes and six foe looks (the raider plus five new) are rigged** on one 25-bone skeleton with ten shared clips (Idle, Walk, Melee, Ranged, Cast, Hit, Downed, Death, Recover, Brace) and a few per-character overrides. Pose precedence: a spell casts; a non-spell attack swings or shoots; a non-spell self or ally effect braces; a potion, rescue, End Turn or move plays none. A non-spell ranged attack is a lightless arrow released at the bow's release frame; spell bolts are class-tinted and released at the Cast frame with their light ramping in. Playback is driven by committed commands and never resolves outcomes.
- **The skirmisher, brute and warden are in play from catalog revision 9** ([#188](https://github.com/AustinKelsay/dnd-diceroll/issues/188), PR A): revision 9 runs save `Role` `Skirmisher`, `Brute` and `Warden`, which `ExpeditionFoe::LookFromSavedRole` already decides, so their rigs draw in real pressure fights and the finale. PR B moves the remaining looks onto the saved Role and checks their `HeightScale` and palette against real encounters. Until the kit reads `FExpeditionRun::Encounter`, recipes 3–6 draw with the stones fallback (`Variants`); the finale keeps the Vault by fight index.
- **Arenas stay 16 to 18 x 11 to 12 cells** (#151 closed). A larger arena would be a new generation revision on top of #188 and #95's seeded Compact baseline.
- **Verification tools** live in `tools/` and are documented in [development](../development.md): `gallery` with pose sets under `tools/capture/` (cast, foes, release, duplicate, palette, overview), `figure_contrast.py`, `palette_report.py`, `accent_share.py`, `impact_flash.py`, `duplicate_sheet.py`, `palette_sheet.py`, `bolt_strip.py`, and `perf` against the budgets below. The gates the overhaul used: a rigged class's contrast mean at least 2.3, worst shot at least 1.4 and every per-board mean at least 2.0 at default zoom on Vigil, Root and the Vault; no caster or shooter box above 2% pure white at release; accent regions 4 to 8% of the visible figure.
- **Perf gate for the overhaul** (1920x1080, packaged Development, warm, `--cores 2`): p95 frame 4 ms, GPU 3.5 ms, game thread 1.5 ms, RSS 1.5 GiB (the helper itself still checks its looser 16.7 / 12 / 4 ms and 5 GiB). At `f09abfe` three default runs gave p95 3.36 to 3.38 / 2.67 to 2.69 / 1.19 to 1.20 ms and 1243 to 1252 MiB, with no steady-state frame over 7 ms and no cluster of slow frames; the gate-2 baseline was 3.15 / 2.49 / 1.19 ms, 1260 MiB and the last release (`0f3042e`) 3.23 / 2.71 / 1.19 ms, 1237 MiB. The report and the capture galleries are linked from the #152 PR.
- **Merge rules that bit during the overhaul.** CI must be green immediately before `gh pr merge` (#203 merged red and #204 fixed it: a list comprehension in a test saw eval locals only on Python 3.12+, and CI runs 3.11, so run helper tests on Python 3.11 too). Two PRs that touch the same files need an integrated build before the second merges (#205 and #206 both touched `CaptureFixtures.cpp`, `docs/assets.md` and `docs/development.md`; their combination was built and tested in #152 and was clean).
- **Remaining limits** are in the run record's [Remaining limits](graphics-overhaul/README.md#remaining-limits-2026-11-12): Mac #32 and #35, the Linux shutdown assertion #157, the mitigated gallery crash #174, physical-pointer and pan/zoom coverage, human balance, rogues from behind, the foes waiting on #188, perf on a headless nested output, Nanite and SM6 (#193 and #105), plus smaller items the PRs recorded.

## Hero details clear of the route, reward and camp panels (#200, 2026-11-12)

A hero's details panel (`DrawPartyDetail`) is drawn before the route banner and cards and the reward and camp panels, so at the HUD's 1280 x 720 design width (1280x720 and 1920x1080 alike) slots 2 to 4 lay under them. The placement is now `ExpeditionLayout::PartyDetailClearOf` in `TargetChipLayout.h`: the panel keeps its place beside its card when that is clear of the phase's block (`RouteBlockRect`, `RewardPanelRect`, `CampPanelRect`, the same rectangles the draws use) and otherwise sits centred under it, above the feedback line, or above or right of it if that does not fit. The overview's `HudRects` hold the placed rectangles, so the dioramas stay clear of them too. Presentation only; no rules, RNG or save change. Tests: `Project.Expedition.Presentation.PartyDetailsKeepClearOfPhasePanels` (geometry, every slot and panel height at 1920x1080, 1280x720 and 1600x600) and `RouteHeroDetailsDrawClearOfBannerAndCards` (the real HUD on the route, reward and camp screens, each hero, natural and tallest). `tools/capture/route-poses.json` captures the four heroes (`"hero": 1..4` on an `overview` shot).
