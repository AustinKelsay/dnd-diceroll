## Anomalies
- **SDL SIGFPE on launch**, once in 9 launches: E's first launch (720p), `handle_wl_output_done`, `logs/game-E-first-launch-sigfpe.log`; the immediate relaunch worked. Known quirk.
- **Ranger dagger visible during the draw** and release-frame blow-out: row 5 above. This is the one defect I would send back or accept as a follow-up.
- **Default-party game thread 1.28-1.29 ms**, 0.1 ms above #197 (row in the perf table).
- My own helper typed named keys (`Escape`, `Return`) as letters on the first attempt in game A; that session was discarded and redone as A2 (those letters acted as hotkeys; A's save was a scratch copy). All reported results are from A2, C, D, E, F, G, H.
- Perf run 3 (default party, 2nd sample) has no warm-up pass of its own, so its report prints "No warm-up pass"; the numbers are from a warm package.
