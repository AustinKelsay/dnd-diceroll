## Notes
- **N1 (rig limit):** `wtype -M shift` and `-P Shift_L` + `s` did not pan the camera (one capture pair differs by 0.35% of pixels, idle animation), the same as in the pr215 pass. The middle-drag pan was used instead.
- **N2 (rig quirk, not a defect): a click only registers if the game has seen the pointer move onto the cell first.** A `ptr m X Y c` (move then click at once), or a move to the pixel the pointer is already on, produced no action, with or without the PR's rule; so every trial wiggles 15 px, moves to the cell and waits 700 ms (the hover preview shows) before pressing. The same quick click that did nothing then moves Sera (still click, 4c). Also, my first slip tests (3 px) failed because the test pixel (1395,483) sits on a **cell boundary** (3 px right = "Move 2 squares", 3 px left = "Move 3 squares", see `m-probe`), so they were correctly cancelled by the "same previewed cell" rule. Re-run at the cell interior (1375,495) they move as expected. The negative tests in 1 to 3 pressed at that same boundary pixel, but their cancel cause is the camera or the 10+ px pointer movement, and a still click at that pixel (4a, 4c) does move, so they stand.
- **N3 (not reachable on this board):** every hero is at full HP and none is downed, so Cure Wounds ("No wounded target"), Rescue and Potion have no legal target; Sculpted Burst's only candidate target was out of range. A human could check these on a board with wounded or downed heroes; they use the same click path as Sanctuary and Magic Missile.
- **N4 (#171 hand-back not cleanly demonstrated):** after a `Left` arrow key, a click preceded by pointer motion moved Arden at once (`m-6kbd`, top row), and two clicks with **no** pointer motion (position unchanged) did nothing either time (bottom row). So I could not show "keyboard use, then first click only hands the board back" with this pointer; no sign that this PR changed it, but a human should confirm #171 once with a real mouse.
- Wait times in the scripts are generous (hold 100 to 700 ms, `sleep` 1.5 to 2 s after release) so the checkpoint JSON has been written before reading.
- Not done: attack mode with an orbit during the press (no enemy in Arden's or Sera's reach in the time available after the first attack), 1280x720, other seeds or party sizes, a physical mouse.
