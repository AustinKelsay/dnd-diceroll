## Spell-bolt release and the casters' basic attack (lead's follow-ups)

- `60-spell-release-before-after-a.jpg`: the caster's footprint at the release frame, left: the full 50 cd light (before), right: the ramped-in light (after). Rows: the Wizard's Fire Bolt on the Vault, the Cleric's radiant bolt on the Vault, the Wizard's Fire Bolt on the default seed; default zoom, yaw 0, 1920x1080.
- `63-caster-release-and-flight-board-vault.jpg`, `63-caster-release-and-flight-default-seed-battle.jpg`: **production-equivalent previews of the sorcerer, warlock and druid's ranged basic attack** (a spell bolt in the class tint: ember red, eldritch green, leaf gold; the shooter held in its Cast clip at the release frame). They are capture `impact` previews that hold the shooter's clip and build the bolt with the production projectile helper (`FExpeditionPlayback::RangedBolt`, the same colour, size and light path as `AddAttack`); they are not a recording of a played attack. The actual playback wiring (the Cast pose, the release-frame launch at 0.73 s, the class tint, a bolt rather than an arrow, Melee up close) is asserted through real `FExpeditionPlayback` by `Project.Expedition.Presentation.CasterBasicAttacks` and `RangedReleaseArrows`. One row per class, left the release (the bolt leaving the caster: no wash on the caster or the floor), right the flight (the coloured light along the path and the bolt's glow intact); default zoom, yaw 0, 1920x1080.
- `62-spell-release-luma.md`: the table per caster and per shot (peak, mean luma, pure-white share before and after, both resolutions) and the in-flight table of the three casters (shooter box and bolt box). `62-raw-impact-flash-*.json`: the raw `tools/impact_flash.py --json` output (`before_a`/`before_b`: ramp off; `after_a`: `spell-release-poses.json` on `default-seed-battle` and `board-vault`; `after_b`: `cast3-release-poses.json` on the cast3 fixtures, with the new flight shots).
- The pure-white share is under 1% in all 108 release boxes (largest 0.65%; it reached 35.2% before) and at most 0.18% in every in-flight caster box. The peak luma stays at 251 to 254.6 in most boxes, the floor of these boxes: the no-light arrow-release controls read 244 to 254.6 from board-grid lines, HP bars and the figures' pale trim (accepted by the lead as scene content).
