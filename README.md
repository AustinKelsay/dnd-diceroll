## Notes and findings
- **N1 (rig limit, not a game defect): Shift+W/A/S/D panning not exercised.** `wtype` held Shift (as `-M` and as `-P Shift_L`) and the game ignored it (zoomed-in frames identical). The brief's middle-drag alternative worked and was used. A human should confirm Shift+S once; the pan bound is the same code path (`LimitPan`).
- **N2 (observation, not a turn pass): a left-button drag released over a legal move cell commits a move if the camera was moving during the drag.** Left-drags on a settled camera (about 8) did nothing. With an orbit key pressed mid-drag, 1 of 2 repeats moved Sera from 4,6 to 2,6 (movement 4 to 2), and an earlier combined run moved her from 4,6 to 5,6. The turn did not pass and the hero was unchanged, so #208's stated check ("a drag never ends a turn") holds, but the release acts as a click on the cell under the pointer (#172 semantics), so a drag ending on a legal cell can spend movement. Possibly worth a look if drags should never command; out of scope for #215.
- **N3 (minor, presentation): zoomed in (not default) at the Vigil's near pan stop, the rose's coloured light pool and a stippled ledge fill the bottom third behind the action bar (06).** The PR's claim is about the default zoom, where it is a sliver (05); a closer zoom plus a near pan can still bring the projected rose light back, because the 0.75-cell bound is the same at every zoom. Cosmetic, dithered, not a regression.
- Not done: Root board at 1280x720 (same design width as 1080, judged equal), Shift-key pan, a physical mouse, other seeds, party sizes or the Vault pillars board, a numeric per-frame opacity measurement.
- Rig quirk: the nested Hyprland lists the host's two DRM monitors as 0x0 stubs; I disabled them in my own instance only. The owner's compositor (pid 1623) was untouched; my compositor, games and lock are gone.
